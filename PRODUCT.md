## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed by Sanjay, 2026-10-26):

- **Builders**: makers, inventors and students hunting for inventions whose protection has ended, so they can build on them for free. They browse, search by idea, read claims and drawings, and take a patent into their own Claude account with the kit.
- **Checkers**: people at small businesses (and anyone about to build, buy or file) cross-checking what is already patented anywhere in the world, in force or not.

Both arrive by search or by a shared patent link at least as often as through the home page.

## Product Purpose

Patent Yard makes two things free that are usually paid or buried: finding inventions anyone may now use, and searching every published patent worldwide. Success is a visitor leaving with either a patent they can build on (and a clear sense of what is free about it) or a confident answer about what already exists.

## Positioning

A free register of patents whose protection has ended, each one traced to the official record it came from and rechecked against that record, sitting beside a free search of 122.8 million patents from 106 offices. Competitors either sell patent search or list "expired patents" without showing why each one is free; Patent Yard shows the rule, the dates and the source for every listing, and hands the patent to the visitor's own Claude to reinvent.

## Operating Context

- Static site generated by a Python pipeline (`pipeline/website.py`) into `web/dist/`, hosted on the Cloudflare Pages free plan; patent pages, drawings and kit data are served by Pages Functions out of packed shard files.
- Two sections: **Free to use** (recently freed US register of ~120k patents plus an archive of 11.7M US, European, Canadian and Australian patents past their term) and **All patents** (122.8M records worldwide, for checking).
- Other pages: Opportunities (analysed picks), Insights (charts), Invention studio, How it works, Saved patents, My bench (account), Settings, legal.
- The site republishes every two hours from a Mac; listings are rechecked daily against USPTO notices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Everything must stay free to run: Cloudflare Pages free plan (20,000 files per project, 25 MiB per file, 10 ms CPU per function request, 100k function requests a day), Supabase free tier for accounts and reservations. No paid services, no ads.
- No AI runs on the site. The "Reinvent with Claude" kit writes a prompt and a PDF brief in the visitor's browser for their own Claude account.
- Reservations are notes to other users only; they never stop anyone from using a patent, and the site must say so wherever reserving appears.
- Weapons patents (classes F41, F42, C06B, C06C, C06D, G21J) get no Claude kit, sketches or reservations.
- Statuses are factual and sourced: "Lapsed, fee not paid", "Expired, full term", "Likely abandoned" (labeled as inferred), "Past its term" for other offices. All patents shows other statuses as published, unchecked.
- Not legal advice; the site says so and links its disclaimer.

## Brand Commitments

- The name **Patent Yard** stays. The logo and wordmark may change.
- Visual reference (Sanjay, 2026-11-02, "Patent Yard v3" brief): Apple-level presentation, typography, controls and motion without copying Apple, with an identity of its own built around the original drawings; a public face with the impact of a product launch, a research application with the usability of a desktop app. v3 is "the light table": originals on lit glass, Patent Yard's notes on vellum, graphite actions, one vermilion mark. Replaces v2 (cool surfaces, blue accent) and the earlier drafting-room and dark technical-instrument directions. Details in DESIGN.md.
- **Reinvent with Claude** (the kit) and the **Invention studio** stay as features.
- The home page's **plotted drawing**: a real patent drawing redrawn by a pen that follows its strokes. It stays as the signature animation.
- A **light and dark** switch stays, alongside the existing reduced-motion, large-text and original-drawings settings.
- Voice: plain and specific. No hype, no em-dash asides, no invented features; say what the patent itself says.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real data: 122,826,228 worldwide records (106 offices); 11.7M archive patents (US 7.15M, Europe 1.83M, Canada 1.90M, Australia 0.81M); ~123k recently freed US patents, about 1,000 with hand-written plain-English notes (`pipeline/curation.json`); 18 analysed opportunity picks (`pipeline/opportunities.json`).
- Original USPTO drawing sheets, cropped (`/img/p/<id>/<n>.png`), for most register patents.
- No testimonials, customers, press or usage numbers exist. Do not invent any, and do not fake reservations or activity.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim of "free to use" shows its rule, its dates and its source.
2. Search first: a visitor with a number or an idea should be one step from an answer.
3. The real patent, not a summary of one: drawings, claims and records are the content.
4. Free to run, forever: every feature fits the free hosting limits.
5. Plain words over patent jargon, without losing accuracy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Existing settings must keep working: reduced motion (site setting and OS preference), large text, light/dark/system theme, original drawings. Keyboard access throughout (search "/", command palette Cmd/Ctrl+K, viewer arrows). Many visitors are students on phones and school Chromebooks.
